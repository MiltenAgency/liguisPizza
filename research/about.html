THE STORY BEHIND US

our story

We started with a simple love for baking and turned it into a little bakery filled with big flavors, sweet ideas, and treats worth sharing

Contact us

Contact us

01
2022 - WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

A small idea, a love for baking, and a kitchen full of sweet experiments started our journey. What began with simple recipes and late-night baking quickly became a passion for creating treats that brought people together. Every cake, pastry, and little experiment helped shape the bakery we dreamed of building.

02
2024 - GROWING CRAFT

We turned our favorite recipes into something bigger, creating cakes, pastries, and treats that people loved coming back for. With every order, we learned, experimented, and refined our craft, adding new flavors while staying true to the homemade care that started it all.

03
2026 - BAKING WHAT’S NEXT

Today, we keep creating new flavors, playful treats, and delicious moments for every kind of craving. From everyday sweet bites to cakes made for special celebrations, everything is baked with the same curiosity and care that started our journey. And as we look ahead, we’re excited to keep experimenting.
